Privacy Policy

Capy’s Plotopia · last updated 4 August 2026

The short version. There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, your email, or your child’s age. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share anything with anyone for advertising.

Who we are

Capy’s Plotopia is made by PXT Tech Studio, LLC. If anything here is unclear, write to support@capybaraaroundtheworld.com.

What the app stores

On your device

  • A library key — sixteen random characters generated on your device the first time you open the app. It is kept in the system keychain (iOS) or keystore (Android). It is not linked to you, your phone, or any account: it simply says “this library is mine”.
  • The nickname you type and your chosen interface language.

On our servers

  • The stories themselves — the text, the choices made, and the setup you gave (genre, language, reading level, any characters or scene you typed).
  • The nickname each player chose, so everyone can see whose turn it is. You can type anything here; it does not have to be a real name, and we would rather it wasn’t a child’s full one.
  • A one-way hash of your library key — never the key itself. A copy of our database could not be used to open anyone’s library.
  • How many twists you have left, and any purchases, against that hash.

What we do not store

No email addresses, no passwords, no phone numbers, no contacts, no location, no advertising identifiers, no device fingerprints. The app contains no analytics or tracking SDK of any kind.

How long things last

  • A story nobody has touched for 30 days is deleted automatically.
  • A story you keep stays in your library until you delete it. Deleting it removes it from our servers.
  • Losing your library key means losing access to that library. We cannot recover it — we do not know who it belonged to. That is the cost of having no accounts, and it is deliberate.

Who else sees this

Writing a story means sending it to a language model. We use these providers, and only for the feature named:

  • Google — text-to-speech, for the “read aloud” button. The passage being read is sent; the audio comes back. It is not used to train anything.
  • Google (Gemini) — writing the story, explaining a word, and translating character descriptions. It receives the story so far and the setup you gave. Your stories are not used to train it.
  • Supabase — our database and server hosting.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms. We do not send them your library key, your balance, or anything identifying you.

If we ever change which model writes the stories, we will update this page and say so in the app. We will not quietly add a provider that receives your stories.

Children

Capy’s Plotopia is made for families to use together, and an adult sets up the story. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children either. There is no chat, no messaging between strangers, and no way to find or contact another player: a story is joined only by a code that someone reads out to you.

Stories are written by a language model. We instruct it to write for the audience and reading level chosen, but as with any such system, we cannot promise every sentence will suit every family. We suggest an adult stays nearby, particularly with younger children.

Playing in a browser

The web version stores your nickname and interface language in your browser. If you type a library key there, it is held only until you close the window — it is never saved to the browser and never put in a link.

Payments

Twists are bought through Apple or Google. We never see your card details. We record only that a purchase happened and how many twists to add.

Your choices

  • Delete any kept story from My library at any time.
  • Leave a story and your nickname is removed from it.
  • Delete the app to remove the library key from your device. Tell us if you would like the matching server data removed too — send the library key from the app’s key panel and we will delete everything held against it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing this page.
